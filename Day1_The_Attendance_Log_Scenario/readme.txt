Mini Project 1 — The Attendance Log

Scenario: You're hired by a small company. They give you a TXT file with the employees and their weekly hours worked. You need to analyze it.

Create the log.txt file:
Marco Rossi: 38
Anna Bianchi: 45
Luca Verdi: 32
Sara Neri: 40
Paolo Gialli: 52
Giulia Bruno: 28        

The program must:
-Read the file
-Create a dictionary {name: hours}
-Print all employees with their hours
-Find who worked the most hours
-Find who worked the fewest hours
-Calculate the average hours for the entire company
-Print who worked overtime — more than 40 hours
